7-Stage BIS Conformity Assessment Roadmap

1
Standard & QCO Identification Confirm applicability of {{ standard.standard }} and verify whether central Gazette notification enforces mandatory ISI marking.
2
In-House Laboratory Infrastructure Setup Establish calibrated factory testing apparatus in accordance with the Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT).
3
Application Dossier on e-BIS ManakOnline Submit Form-I on www.manakonline.in along with factory layout, manufacturing machinery list, and test personnel qualifications.
4
Factory Audit & Sample Drawing BIS investigating officers conduct a 2-day on-site production audit and draw counter-samples for independent testing in recognized NABL labs.
5
Grant of 7-Digit CM/L License Following satisfactory test reports and payment of statutory marking fees (with 50% MSME concession), BIS authorizes the ISI Mark.
