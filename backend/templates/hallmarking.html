Consumer Rights Statutory ₹45 Testing Fee

Under the Bureau of Indian Standards (Hallmarking) Regulations, any consumer can walk into an accredited Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC) to verify the purity of their jewellery for a statutory fee of ₹45 per article (plus GST). AHCs are legally required to provide an authentic test certificate.

Legal Framework

Statutory Consumer Protection under BIS Act, 2016

Know your rights when purchasing gold or silver ornaments in India.

1

2x Shortage Compensation

If testing at a recognized AHC reveals lower purity than marked on the jewellery, the jeweller is legally obligated to compensate you two times the shortage calculated on the invoice date gold weight and reimburse the ₹45 testing fee.

2

Mandatory Invoice Breakdown

The sales bill must explicitly specify: (1) Gross weight, (2) Net weight, (3) Purity karatage/fineness, (4) Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) 6-digit code, and (5) ₹45 statutory hallmarking charge.

3

Penal Provisions

Selling unhallmarked jewellery in notified districts or counterfeiting the BIS mark is punishable under Section 29 with imprisonment up to 1 year or a fine not less than ₹1,00,000 up to five times the value of goods.
