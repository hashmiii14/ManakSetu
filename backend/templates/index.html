  • Compressive Strength (28d): ≥ 33 / 53 MPa
  • Soundness (Le-Chatelier): ≤ 10 mm
  • Initial Setting Time: ≥ 30 min (Final ≤ 600 min)
  • Standard Consistency: 28% - 32%
