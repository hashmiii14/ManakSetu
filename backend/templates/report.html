Statutory Penalties (BIS Act, 2016)

Under Section 29 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, any person who manufactures, distributes, sells, or imports goods bearing a counterfeit standard mark or violates a mandatory Quality Control Order (QCO) is liable to:

  • Imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years.
  • Fine of not less than two lakh rupees (which may extend up to ten times the value of manufactured goods).
  • Confiscation and destruction of substandard commercial consignments.
