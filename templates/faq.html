Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common inquiries regarding Bureau of Indian Standards certification, mandatory QCOs, MSME fee concessions, and hallmarking

Citizen & Industry Guide

What is the difference between an Indian Standard (IS) and a Quality Control Order (QCO)?

An Indian Standard (IS) is a technical specification published by the Bureau of Indian Standards that prescribes quality, safety, and performance criteria for a product. By default, adherence is voluntary unless the Central Government issues a Quality Control Order (QCO) under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016. Once a QCO is notified in the Gazette of India, manufacturing, importing, stocking, or selling that item without the standard ISI Mark becomes a criminal offence punishable under Section 29.

How does a Micro or Small Enterprise claim the 50% / 20% marking fee concession?

Under the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, Micro enterprises and women-led startups qualify for a 50% concession on the minimum marking fee, and Small enterprises qualify for a 20% concession. To claim this relief, the applicant must upload a valid Udyam Registration Certificate issued by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises during application filing on the e-BIS ManakOnline portal.

How can a consumer verify whether an ISI mark on cement or an appliance is authentic?

Every genuine ISI marked article must carry a 7-digit license number stamped directly below the mark in the format CM/L-XXXXXXX. You can verify this number instantly using the TrueMark Verifier on ManakSetu or via the official BIS CARE mobile application. If the license is suspended, expired, or non-existent, you can file a grievance docket through our Grievance Portal.

What are the specific packaging requirements for cement under IS 1489 and IS 12269?

BIS enforces strict packaging color coding to prevent deceptive commercial substitution:

  • Portland Pozzolana Cement (IS 1489 Part 1 - PPC): All bag markings, IS code, and licensee details must be printed in RED ink.
  • 53 Grade Ordinary Portland Cement (IS 12269 - OPC): All bag markings must be printed in BLACK ink.

What is a 6-character HUID code in gold hallmarking?

A Hallmarking Unique Identification (HUID) is a 6-digit alphanumeric code laser-engraved onto every piece of gold jewellery at a BIS-recognized Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC). The HUID guarantees individual item traceability, jeweler accreditation, and assayed purity (e.g. 22K / 916 fineness).
