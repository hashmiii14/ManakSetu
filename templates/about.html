About ManakSetu Portal

Bridging Indian industry, MSME manufacturers, and consumers with the Bureau of Indian Standards

SIH 2026 • Problem Statement 26107

Project Purpose & Hackathon Mandate

ManakSetu ("The Standards Bridge") is an educational civic-tech platform developed for the Smart India Hackathon 2026 under Problem Statement 26107, championed by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

Navigating over 21,000 Indian Standards, complex Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and multi-tier fee structures has historically presented a steep compliance barrier for small enterprises. ManakSetu solves this challenge through an integrated suite of digital services:

  • Hybrid Standards Retrieval: Combines lexical BM25 ranking, domain query expansions, and vector embeddings across 572 indexed BIS standards.
  • Grounded ManakBot AI: RAG engine using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash grounded strictly in the BIS compendium with zero AI hallucination.
  • MSME 50% Concession Calculator: Transparent tariff computation under Section 13 of the BIS Act, calculating real savings (e.g. ₹92,500 on cement).
  • TrueMark Dual Verifier: Citizen verification of 7-digit ISI license numbers (CM/L) and 6-character gold jewelry hallmarks (HUID).
  • Statutory Grievance Redressal: Violation reporting generating unique tracking dockets integrated with National Consumer Helpline standards.

Statutory Foundation

The Bureau of Indian Standards is the National Standards Body of India established under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016.

Key Statutory Mandates:
  • Harmonious development of standardization activities
  • Conformity assessment and product certification (ISI Mark)
  • Mandatory hallmarking of gold and silver articles
  • Protection of consumer health and public safety

Prototype Disclaimer

This application was built exclusively as an academic and technological submission for the Smart India Hackathon. It does not replace statutory filings or certification procedures administered through the official manakonline.in portal.
