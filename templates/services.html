Bureau of Indian Standards Services Directory

Overview of statutory schemes, conformity assessment programs, and certification wings operated by BIS

Conformity Assessment Schemes
Scheme-I

Product Certification Scheme (ISI Mark)

The flagship certification scheme of BIS under which manufacturers are authorized to apply the standard ISI Mark on products conforming to specified Indian Standards. Involves initial factory inspection and independent testing.

Explore Scheme-I Standards →
Scheme-II

Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)

Mandatory registration for IT, electronics, solar inverters, and telecom equipment based on self-declaration of conformity supported by test reports from BIS-recognized testing laboratories.

Visit CRS Portal →
Scheme-IV

Gold & Silver Hallmarking Scheme

Statutory verification of precious metal purity. Hallmarked articles feature the BIS emblem, purity in karats/fineness (e.g. 22K916), and a 6-character laser Hallmarking Unique Identification (HUID) code.

Verify HUID Code →
FMCS

Foreign Manufacturers Certification

Certification mechanism enabling overseas production plants to export mandatory quality goods into India. Requires appointment of an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) and on-site audit.

FMCS e-Filing →
Testing

Laboratory Recognition Scheme (LRS)

Accreditation and statutory recognition of independent commercial testing laboratories across India under ISO/IEC 17025 for testing conformity assessment counter-samples.

View Recognized Labs →
MSME Concessions

MSME & Startup Tariff Support

Targeted financial relief under the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations offering 50% discount on marking fees for micro-enterprises and women entrepreneurs, and 20% discount for small industrial enterprises.
